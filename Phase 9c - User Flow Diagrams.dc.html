Band B · Onboarding

Band D · Commerce, team & settings

Four flows entered on purpose

F14 Upgrade, add-on & overage

Three entry points, one destination. The rule: never interrupt measurement to collect money.

ENTRY 1 · A BLUR
Clicking a locked region carries the context to checkout ("unlock competitor names on 41 queries"), not a generic pricing page.
ENTRY 2 · A METER
Quota banner at 85% (meter().state = near) and again when over. Over ≠ blocked: the scan runs, the block is billed at cycle end.
ENTRY 3 · SETTINGS
/settings/billing — plan, meters, add-ons, invoices in one pane.
SYSTEM api/billing/checkout · portal · addon
Provider-hosted checkout, webhook flips the plan. Price IDs are Phase 8 placeholders returning 501.
◆ DOWNGRADE
Names what will be lost using their own numbers ("312 of your 500 queries stop being tracked; 4 saved views become read-only"), then takes effect at period end. History is retained, not deleted.
✓ Returns to the exact screen that triggered it, unlocked, scrolled to the same row.

F17 Settings — profile, sessions, notifications, brand

Low-frequency, high-consequence. Each pane saves independently; nothing here has a wizard.

/settings · PROFILE
Name, timezone (drives the Monday 08:00 send), and email change — which re-verifies via the same token flow as F04.
/settings · SESSIONS
Device, location, last seen, revoke. Revoking the current session signs you out and says so first.
/settings/email · RULES
Per membership: digest, scan-complete, fix-verified, rate-drop with a threshold slider (default 5 points), quota warnings, Slack webhook. Alerts still only fire outside the band — the threshold narrows, it cannot override the statistics.
/settings/brand
Logo, one accent colour, client-facing footer line. Live PDF preview, because P3 will not risk finding out at send time.
CONSTRAINT
The accent replaces --cited only. Ink, paper, the SURFACE/PROXY marks and the methodology block are not re-mappable — they carry meaning, not decoration.
