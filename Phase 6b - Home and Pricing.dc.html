03Cause separation

Not all lost clicks have the same cause. The fix depends on which one is yours.

AIO displacement The answer replaced the click You still rank. An AI answer sits above you and cites someone else. Fixed with citable content, not with SEO.
Ranking decline You actually slipped A plain position loss, hiding inside the same falling line. Your existing SEO playbook applies — and only here.
Technical decay Engines can't read you Broken pages, blocked crawlers, missing structure. Cheap to fix, invisible until something measures it.
Other / unexplained Honestly labeled What the model can't attribute stays striped and named "unexplained" — never silently folded into a cause.
04Method

A number you can defend in the meeting where someone asks how it's calculated.

Every query is asked on every engine, every run. Citations are matched to your domain by resolved URL, not string similarity. Causes are attributed per query, and anything below confidence stays "unexplained." The method page follows one real query end to end.

Twelve reasons people stall, answered.

What exactly is a tracked query?One prompt a buyer would ask, checked on every engine in your plan, at your plan's refresh rate. 100 queries on Starter means 100 prompts — engines and frequency don't multiply the count.
Which engines do you check?ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews on every plan; Perplexity and Gemini from Growth; Copilot and Claude on Scale and above. Coverage changes are flagged in the changelog.
Where do the queries come from?We propose a set from your site, your Search Console data if connected, and your category. You edit everything — swap, add, or retire queries whenever you like.
Is there a trial?The free scan is the trial — a real reading on your real domain, no account. Paid plans start with your scan history intact, so nothing is measured twice.
What happens when I hit my limit?Scans keep running. You see a banner, and the overage block ($19 per 100 queries) is billed at the end of the cycle. We never stop measuring mid-month.
Why are competitor names locked on Starter?Starter answers "do I have a problem." Naming who benefits is the analysis layer, and it's the honest line between the two plans — not an artificial crippling of the data.
Can I cancel or downgrade anytime?Yes, from billing settings, effective at the end of the cycle. Your history is exportable for 90 days after cancellation.
How is the citation rate calculated?Cited answer runs divided by total answer runs, per query, per engine. Citations are matched by resolved URL. The method page shows one query end to end.
Do competitors know I track them?No. We read public engine answers; we never touch competitor properties. They appear because engines cite them, not because we crawled them.
Can I pay by invoice?Card on all self-serve plans, handled by our merchant of record with VAT and sales tax included. Invoicing is available on Enterprise.
What about my data?Your query set and results are yours; we never train on them or show them to anyone else. DPA and subprocessor list are on the legal pages, signed DPA on Enterprise.
Do you support non-English markets?Queries can be in any language the engines answer. Cause attribution currently performs best in English; we say so rather than pretend otherwise.
