about

We built the instrument we wanted to be handed.

A measurement product is only worth the trust you place in the people making the measurement. So here's who we are and what we've committed to.

In 2025, the reports stopped making sense. Impressions held, positions held, clicks fell. Every tool on the market drew the same falling line and none of them could say why — because the cause wasn't in the data any of them collected. The answer above the links had started citing three sources, and you were either one of them or you weren't.
Citerate exists to measure that one thing carefully, and to say plainly when it can't. It is deliberately not an AI content generator, not a rank tracker with a new label, and not a dashboard that reframes uncertainty as insight.

What we measure, and what it means

Every post here is built on scan data we actually ran. Where the sample is thin, we say the sample is thin.
2026-06-3011 minresearch

Ranking #1 and cited 0% of the time

We took 200 domains across eight categories, each holding a top-three organic position on at least ten commercial queries. For every domain we ran its own top ten queries through four engines, three runs each — 24,000 answer runs in total. Then we asked one question of the results: when you already win the link, do you also win the answer?
Fifty-one of the 200 domains — just over a quarter — recorded a citation rate of zero on their own winning queries. Not low. Zero. These are sites with authority, traffic, and a rank tracker showing green.
Every one of those fifty-one domains had a dashboard that said everything was fine.

method

How the rate is computed

The citation rate is the share of answers, across your query set and the engines you track, in which your domain is named or linked. It is computed from raw answers on every run — never from an earlier rate.
rate = cited_answers / total_answers
band = 1.96 * sqrt(rate * (1 - rate) / total_answers)
A change smaller than the band is reported as flat, not as movement. This is the single most important line on this page.
