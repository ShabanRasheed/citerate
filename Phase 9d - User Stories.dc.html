Epic 1 · Acquisition & the free scan

Prove the problem is real before asking for anything

flows F01 F02 · marketing repo
5 stories · 5 must
US-01 MUST P2 F01 · /

AS A founder who suspects he is invisible to AI answers, I WANT to see my real citation rate by typing only my domain, SO THAT I can judge whether this matters to me before spending anything — including my email address.

GIVEN I am on the marketing home page and have never visited before, WHEN I enter a resolvable hostname and submit, THEN a scan starts with no account, no card and no email required.
AND results stream in progressively — the first engine's outcome appears without waiting for the last.
AND 25 queries across 2 engines are run, and the count is stated on screen so the sample size is never implied.
AND if the hostname does not resolve, the error appears inline beneath the field with my input preserved — no page navigation, no lost typing.
US-02 MUST P2 P1 F01 · /scan/[token]

AS A visitor with ninety seconds of attention, I WANT the finding stated as one plain sentence before any chart, SO THAT I understand the result without interpreting a dashboard.

GIVEN a scan has completed, WHEN the readout renders, THEN the first element is a serif sentence naming the host, the cited count and the run count.
AND every rate on the page is rendered as cited/runs — pct; a bare percentage anywhere fails this story.
AND each engine carries a visible SURFACE or PROXY label wherever its number appears.
US-03MUSTP1 · /how-it-works

As an SEO manager who will be cross-examined on this number, I want the full method published before I sign up, so that I can decide whether it is defensible.

Method page states the run-based rate, the Wilson band, the three causes, the confidence floor and the current method version — and every score in the product links to it.
US-04MUSTP2 · /scan/[token]

As a visitor, I want to see that competitor names exist in my result even while they're hidden, so that I know exactly what I'd be paying for.

Locked region renders my real data blurred with a dashed border and a count ("3 competitors cited"), never an empty placeholder or a marketing panel.
US-05MUSTP2 · F01

As a visitor whose scan is slow, I want the finished part now and the rest emailed, so that a slow engine doesn't cost me the answer.

After 20s, completed engines render with their reduced denominator visible, incomplete ones are labelled still running, and an email option appears carrying the same token.
Epic 2 · Identity without passwords

One token type, two redemption paths, zero passwords

flows F02–F05 · auth.ts session.ts
5 stories · 4 must · 1 could
US-06MUSTP2F02 · /claim/[token]

AS A visitor who just saw a real result, I WANT claiming that scan to also create my account and verify my domain, SO THAT I never re-enter what I already gave you.

GIVEN an unclaimed scan and my email, WHEN I verify, THEN a user, workspace and verified domain are created and the 25 queries plus their runs migrate intact.
AND I land on onboarding step 2 with data already on screen — step 1 is marked complete, not re-asked.
AND if that email already has an account, this becomes a sign-in that attaches the scan as an additional domain, subject to the plan's domain limit.
US-07MUSTP2 P3F04 · /check-email

AS A user on a phone whose mail app opens links in a webview I don't trust, I WANT to type a code instead, SO THAT I can finish signing in on the device I started on.

GIVEN an auth email, THEN it contains both a link and a 6-digit code that are the same token — consuming either invalidates the other.
AND the code field is focused on arrival, accepts paste, and auto-submits on the sixth character.
AND resend is available on a 30-second cooldown with the remaining seconds shown.
US-08MUSTP3 · F04

As an agency owner signing in dozens of times a month, I want to stay signed in and see my sessions, so that passwordless doesn't mean an email round trip every day.

Long-lived session cookie; a valid session lands directly on the last-visited route. Every session is listed with device, location and last-seen, and is individually revocable.
US-09MUSTall · F18

As a user who opened the email an hour late, I want a one-tap recovery, so that an expired link is not a dead end.

Expiry states the 15-minute TTL, pre-fills the email, and re-mints on one tap. Any attached scan survives because it is keyed to the email, not the token. Unknown emails never reveal whether an account exists.
US-10MUSTP3 · /invite/[token]

As an invited colleague or client, I want to land inside the right workspace already set up, so that I never see onboarding for someone else's account.

Accepting creates a membership at the invited role and lands on /overview. A revoked or expired invite names the inviter and offers to notify them. Role client sees only Overview and Reports.
Epic 3 · Activation

Get to a real number with the least possible input

flows F06–F08 · 5 stories
3 must · 2 should
US-11MUSTP1 P3F06 · /onboarding/domain

AS A new user, I WANT three ways to prove I own the domain, SO THAT I am not blocked by whichever one I lack access to.

GIVEN a hostname, THEN DNS TXT, a meta tag, or a connected GSC property all satisfy verification.
AND hostnames are normalised (protocol, www, trailing slash, case) before any comparison.
AND if proof isn't in place yet I can still proceed and browse; verification is required only before the first scheduled scan and before any report. I am told which, plainly.
US-13MUSTP1F07 · /onboarding/queries

AS AN SEO manager, I WANT to review and reshape the generated query set in bulk, SO THAT the numbers reflect prompts my buyers actually type.

GIVEN a verified domain, THEN 25 seeded queries are pre-populated and grouped into topic clusters.
AND I can bulk-paste, inline-add, delete, and re-tag clusters without leaving the step.
AND a live counter shows usage against the plan limit; exceeding it displays the overage cost inline and never blocks the save.
US-12MUSTP1 · join_requests

As a new colleague at a company already using CiteRate, I want to request access rather than hit a wall, so that a collision becomes an introduction.

A pending request is created with a 7-day expiry, admins are notified, I see a clear pending state, and I can add a different domain meanwhile. We never disclose who owns the host beyond the email domain.
US-14MUSTP2 · F07 F08

As a time-poor founder, I want to skip everything optional, so that I reach a number in under a minute of input.

Steps 2 and 3 each offer a primary-weight skip ("Use these 25 for now"), not a grey link. Skipping never leaves a nag banner in more than one place, and both are resumable from Settings.
US-15SHOULDP1 · api/connect/gsc

As an SEO manager, I want to connect Search Console read-only, so that ranking decline is separated from AIO displacement rather than pooled into "unexplained".

Exact scope stated before the redirect. Granting also satisfies domain verification. Declining is a first-class path, and the resulting loss of precision is named on Overview — once.
Epic 4 · Diagnosis

Turn a rate into a believed, actionable finding

flows F09 F11 · scoring.ts
6 stories · 5 must · 1 should
US-18MUSTP1 P3F09 · /overview · confidenceBand()highest-value story in the product

AS A professional who will present this number to someone senior, I WANT the product to tell me when a movement is statistically meaningless, SO THAT I never build a story on noise.

GIVEN two periods with computed Wilson intervals, WHEN the intervals overlap, THEN the delta is labelled within noise everywhere it appears — Overview, Queries, digest email and report alike.
AND no score renders without its band; a bare score is a failing build.
AND rate-drop alerts fire only when the movement is outside the band and past my threshold — the threshold can narrow what I hear, never override the statistics.
AND a within-noise week produces no task and no manufactured urgency; the session is allowed to end.
US-19MUSTP1F09 · drawer

AS AN analyst, I WANT to see the individual runs behind any rate, SO THAT I can re-derive the number myself.

THEN the drawer lists every run with engine, timestamp, cited / mentioned / absent, whether an AI answer was present, and which competitor hosts were cited.
AND the assigned cause is shown with its confidence, or the striped unexplained marker when below the 0.6 floor.
AND it is a drawer, not a route — closing returns me to my exact scroll position and filter.
AND evidence is never plan-gated. Only names and remediation are.
US-17MUSTP1F09 · causeMix()

AS AN SEO manager, I WANT my uncited queries split by cause with the unknown portion shown, SO THAT I can tell an AI problem from an ordinary SEO problem.

THEN the bar shows four segments — AIO displacement, ranking decline, technical decay, other / unexplained — and the fourth is always present, striped, even at 0%.
AND hue is never the only signal: each segment carries a texture or label so the chart survives greyscale print and colour-vision deficiency.
AND clicking a segment navigates to Queries pre-filtered to that cause, sorted by delta.
US-16MUSTP2 · /overview

As any user opening the app, I want one sentence telling me where I stand, so that I am not asked to interpret widgets.

Fixed order: verdict sentence → score with band → cause bar → trend. Scanning and empty states occupy the same slot with the same hierarchy — never a spinner over a blank grid.
US-20SHOULDP1 · saved_views

As an analyst with 500 tracked queries, I want to name and save a filter set, so that my Monday check is two minutes instead of twenty.

Cluster, cause, engine and sort are captured as one named view, optionally shared with the workspace, and deep-linkable so the digest email can point at it.
US-21MUSTP1 P3 · /competitors

As a marketer building a business case, I want the named hosts cited instead of mine on my queries, so that I have the slide that unlocks budget.

Ranked by citations won on my tracked set — never a generic market-share figure. Each host drills to the queries and the per-run receipts. Below Growth: counts visible, names blurred over my own data.
Epic 5 · Remediation & proof

Close the loop, or admit that it didn't close

flows F10 F12 · Growth+
6 stories · 4 must · 2 should
US-22MUSTP1F10 · /fixes

AS AN SEO manager with one sprint slot, I WANT the queue ordered by what it would recover against what it costs, SO THAT the top item is genuinely the right thing to do first.

THEN each item shows estimated recoverable citations with a band and an effort class, and the sort is that ratio by default.
AND technical-decay fixes rank above all else by rule, because unreadable pages invalidate every other diagnosis.
AND queries whose cause is unexplained never generate a fix — we do not invent work to fill the queue.
US-25MUSTP1 P3F10 · isWithinNoise()

AS SOMEONE who shipped a change, I WANT the product to verify it for me against the band, SO THAT the proof is the tool's word and not mine.

GIVEN a fix marked shipped, WHEN two subsequent scans have re-measured the flagged queries, THEN a verdict is issued.
AND the two-scan latency is stated at the moment I mark it shipped — no fake instant confirmation.
AND verified fires an email and makes a fix-proof snapshot available.
US-26MUSTP1 P3F10 · the integrity story

AS A user whose fix did nothing, I WANT to be told that plainly, SO THAT I can trust the times it says the fix worked.

THEN overlapping bands after a fix yield no measurable change — never "failed", never silence.
AND I am offered two more scans of observation rather than a dead end.
AND a fix that made the rate worse is reported as a regression with the same rigour, and its template is down-weighted for everyone.
US-23MUSTP1F10 · FixCard

AS SOMEONE who must convince a developer, I WANT each fix to name the URL, the change and the expected recovery, SO THAT I can paste it into a ticket unedited.

THEN the card carries the exact URL, the concrete change, the queries it should recover, and the evidence link back to the drawer.
AND "copy for ticket" produces plain text that reads correctly with no CiteRate context.
AND dismissing requires a reason, which feeds template weighting.
US-24MUSTP1 · api/fixes/[id]

As an editor, I want to record when the change went live, so that verification compares the right periods.

Marking shipped records a deploy timestamp I can back-date, flags the affected queries for re-test, and states when the verdict will arrive. Viewers and clients don't see the control at all — it is absent, not disabled.
US-27SHOULDP1 · api/rescan

As an impatient user, I want to trigger a scan now, so that I'm not waiting a day to see whether a change landed.

Runs natively on Scale+, otherwise offers a $15/10 pack with the price shown before the click. Progress lives in the topbar; the page stays usable. If one rescan cannot resolve a within-band tie, we say so before spending the credit.
Epic 6 · Reporting

A deliverable an agency can put its own name on

flow F13 · report_snapshots · R2
4 stories · 3 must · 1 could
US-30MUSTP3F13 · the trust guarantee

AS AN agency owner who has already presented last month's number, I WANT it to be impossible for that number to change, SO THAT my credibility does not depend on your release schedule.

GIVEN a snapshot, THEN it renders from its stored payload and is never recomputed — the method version that produced it is printed on it.
AND when the method version changes, charts spanning the change render a flagged discontinuity linking the changelog — history is annotated, never rewritten.
AND one snapshot per domain / period / kind is enforced at the database level.
US-29MUSTP3F13 · /settings/brand · Scale+

AS AN agency owner, I WANT the PDF to carry my brand, SO THAT I can send it to a client without explaining who you are.

THEN my logo, one accent colour and a client-facing footer line are applied, with a live preview before I send anything.
AND the accent replaces the cited colour only; ink, paper, the SURFACE/PROXY marks and the methodology block are locked because they carry meaning.
AND exactly one 10.5px footer line references the methodology; it is not removable, and that is stated in the pricing copy so it is never a surprise.
US-28MUSTP3 · /reports

As an agency owner with 14 clients, I want monthly reports to already exist, so that my month-end is review work and not production work.

Snapshots freeze automatically on the 1st for every domain, with no user action. The index lists period, kind and method version. An optional per-report note is the only writing required.
US-31COULDP3 · deferred

As an agency owner, I want reports delivered straight to my client's inbox on a schedule, so that I don't forward fourteen emails.

Deferred past v1 deliberately: sending mail as an agency needs domain authentication, and getting that wrong damages their deliverability, not ours. Manual send is the honest v1 answer.

US-32MUSTP3F16 · topbar switcher

AS AN agency owner doing the same task for 14 clients, I WANT to switch client and stay on the same screen, SO THAT switching costs me one keystroke, not a re-navigation.

THEN the switcher is in the shell, type-ahead, keyboard-reachable, and shows each client's current score inline for triage before the click.

US-34MUSTP1 P3 · /settings/team

As an admin, I want invites, pending requests and members in one list, so that access is one screen and not a hunt.

US-35SHOULDP3 · /settings/workspaces

As an agency owner onboarding a new client, I want a workspace created from a template, so that client fifteen costs me two minutes.

Name + domain, brand inherited from the agency default, staff seats copied from a template, notification prefs seeded off. 25 included on Agency, then $29 — never a hard cap.
Epic 8 · Commerce & limits

Never interrupt measurement to collect money

flow F14 · plans.ts
4 stories · 3 must · 1 should
US-37MUSTP3F14 · meter()

AS A customer who went over my query limit, I WANT measurement to continue and the overage billed, SO THAT a billing event never puts an unrecoverable hole in my series.

GIVEN usage above the plan limit, THEN the scan still runs and a banner states the exact overage cost in $19 / 100-query blocks.
AND a warning appears at 85% of the limit, before I am over.
AND three recoveries are one click each: buy blocks, upgrade, or trim the query set.
AND usage is read from counters, never counted live over observations.
US-38MUSTP2F14 · Locked.astro

AS A user hitting a locked feature, I WANT the upgrade to be about the thing I just tried to do, SO THAT I am buying an answer rather than a plan.

THEN locked regions render my real data, blurred behind a dashed border with a lock badge — never a marketing panel in place of content.
AND the upsell names the specific unlock in my own numbers ("competitor names on 41 queries").
AND after checkout I return to the exact screen and scroll position that triggered it, unlocked.
US-39MUSTall · F14 downgrade

As a customer considering a downgrade, I want to be told what I lose in my own numbers, so that I am not surprised after the fact.

The confirmation quantifies the loss ("312 of your 500 queries stop being tracked; 4 saved views become read-only"), takes effect at period end, and retains history rather than deleting it.
US-40SHOULDall · F18

As a customer whose card failed, I want a stated grace period, so that an expired card doesn't cost me a month of data.

Grace window shown with a date. Scans continue and data stays readable throughout. Two reminders before anything degrades, each with a portal deep link.

Definition of done

The nine gates every story passes, not just its own criteria

DATA INTEGRITY
  • Every rate shown with its run count
  • Every estimate shown with its band
  • Unexplained segment present even at 0%
DISCLOSURE
  • SURFACE / PROXY label travels with every engine figure
  • Method version reachable from any score
  • Placeholders never silently return fake data
STATES
  • Loading, empty, partial, error, locked and over-quota all designed
  • Every dead end has a named recovery edge
  • Gated controls are absent, never disabled
PERMISSIONS
  • Resolved server-side on every request
  • Hand-typed URL matches the missing-link behaviour
  • Client role never sees agency machinery
ACCESS
  • Keyboard path proven end to end
  • Non-colour encoding on every chart
  • Focus returns where it came from
COPY
  • Findings as sentences, not adjectives
  • No manufactured urgency on a within-noise week
  • Errors say what happened and what to do
