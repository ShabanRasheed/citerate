Appendix · methodology NOT REMOVABLE

How these numbers were produced

Rates, not snapshots

Each query was submitted to each engine 9 times in a randomised order over 31 minutes, with no personalisation, no login, and a fixed locale (en-GB). A citation counts when the answer names the domain or links to it. The reported rate is citations ÷ runs, always printed with both numbers.

Confidence bands

A 9-run rate is an estimate. Bands are Wilson score intervals at 90%; the score band combines those across engines and queries. A band of ±6 means a repeat scan today would very likely land between 56 and 68.

Surface vs proxy

Google AI Overviews and Perplexity were measured on the consumer surface. ChatGPT and Gemini were measured through their APIs (gpt-4.1, web tool on; gemini-2.5, grounding on) because their consumer apps cannot be measured reliably at volume. Proxy numbers are labelled everywhere they appear and are never averaged with surface numbers.

Click-loss estimate

Search Console impressions and clicks for the same 90 days are compared against expected click-through at the observed positions. The residual is apportioned across the four causes; whatever cannot be attributed stays in "other / unexplained" rather than being distributed.

What this report cannot tell you
Whether a citation produced a visit — engines do not report that. What any engine will do next week. Whether a competitor's advantage comes from something we cannot observe, such as a licensing agreement. 18% of the measured click loss is unexplained, and that figure is printed rather than absorbed.
