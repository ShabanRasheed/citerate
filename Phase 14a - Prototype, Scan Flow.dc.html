What it is not
Not a ranking. Position and citation are measured separately and reported separately.
Not a live feed. Engines are sampled on a weekly schedule; a run is a run, dated and immutable.
Not an average of averages. Every rate is computed from raw answers, so a method change never silently rewrites history — it is flagged on the chart instead.
