## When the spreadsheet is actually fine

A one-off curiosity check on five queries? Ask the engines yourself — you don't need us. The spreadsheet stops being fine when you need the same measurement every week, when variance matters, or when someone will challenge the number. If that's not you yet, bookmark this page and go build things.
