## Do it anyway, first

Open an engine and ask the question your best customer would ask. If you're in the answer, good — you have a baseline instinct. If you're not, you now know the shape of the problem.

What you don't have is a number anyone will accept in a meeting, or a way to know next month whether your work moved it. That is the entire gap this product fills.
