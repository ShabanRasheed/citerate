## We are not asking you to cancel anything

Position data is one of the four pieces of evidence our attribution uses. A drop with a held position is displacement; a drop with a fallen position is a ranking problem and your existing playbook applies.

The honest pitch is narrow: if your traffic is falling while your rank tracker stays green, the explanation is not in your rank tracker.
