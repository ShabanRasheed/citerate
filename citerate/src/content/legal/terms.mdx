### 1. The service

Citerate measures how often AI engines cite a domain you control or are authorised to act for, attributes causes, and reports findings. Coverage of specific engines changes over time and is published on the [changelog](/changelog).

### 2. Your account

You are responsible for the accuracy of your account details, for the security of your credentials, and for the actions of users you invite. You confirm you are authorised to run measurements for every domain you add.

### 3. Acceptable use

Do not attempt to overwhelm the free scan, resell access outside the Agency plan, reverse-engineer the attribution model for a competing product, or use the service to attack or misrepresent a third party. We may suspend an account that does, with notice where possible.

### 4. Fees and billing

Plans are billed in advance, monthly or annually. Overage on tracked queries is billed at the end of the cycle in blocks of 100. Payments are processed by our merchant of record, who acts as the seller of record and handles applicable VAT and sales tax. Prices may change with 30 days' notice; your current period is never repriced mid-term.

### 5. What we do not promise

We measure a moving system. We do not guarantee that any engine will cite you, that a fix will improve a rate, or that engine behaviour will stay stable. We do commit to stating our method, publishing its limitations, and flagging any change that affects your history.

### 6. Your data

Your query sets, results, and raw evidence remain yours. We process them to run the service, per the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy) and, where applicable, a signed DPA. We do not train models on your data.

### 7. Termination

You may cancel at any time, effective at the end of the current period. Your history stays exportable for 90 days after cancellation. We may terminate for material breach or non-payment after written notice.

### 8. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, our aggregate liability is limited to the fees you paid in the twelve months before the claim. We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including business decisions taken on the basis of a measurement.
