### 1. Who we are

Citerate is operated by [legal entity name], registered at [address]. For privacy questions, write to privacy@citerate.com. Where this policy refers to "you", it means the account holder and, where applicable, the workspace they belong to.

### 2. What we collect

Account data: name, email, workspace membership, authentication events and trusted devices. Product data: domains, query sets, scan results, raw engine answer text, findings and their states. Billing data is held by our merchant of record; we store only the plan, status, and last four digits.

| Category | Purpose | Retention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account data | Access, security, support | Life of account + 30d |
| Scan results | The product itself | Per plan history limit |
| Raw answer text | Citation evidence, audits | 13 months |
| Free scan results | Serving your share link | 90 days, then deleted |

### 3. How we use it

To run the scans you ask for, to show you the results, to bill you, and to keep the service secure. We also compute aggregate, de-identified statistics across accounts for published research — never in a form that identifies a domain or a customer. You can opt out of aggregate research in workspace settings.

### 4. Who we share it with

Only the subprocessors we depend on to operate: infrastructure and storage, transactional email, payment processing, and bot protection. Each is listed with its purpose and location on the [subprocessors page](/legal/subprocessors), which is versioned — material additions are announced 30 days in advance.

### 5. Your rights

Access, correction, export, deletion, and objection, exercisable from workspace settings or by writing to privacy@citerate.com. We respond within 30 days. Export is machine-readable, includes raw evidence, and does not require a support conversation.

### 6. International transfers

Data is processed on Cloudflare's global network. Standard Contractual Clauses cover transfers out of the EEA and UK. Data residency commitments are available on Enterprise.

### 7. Changes to this policy

Material changes are announced by email to account owners at least 14 days before they take effect, and every version stays listed at the foot of this page.
