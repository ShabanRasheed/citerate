### 1. Roles

For personal data contained in your account and workspace, you act as controller and Citerate acts as processor. For our own business records (billing contacts, support correspondence) we act as controller under the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy).

### 2. Scope of processing

Subject matter: provision of AI-visibility measurement. Duration: the term of your subscription plus retention periods stated in the privacy policy. Categories of data subject: your personnel and the users you invite. Categories of data: names, work emails, authentication metadata, and the content of query sets you create.

### 3. Our obligations

We process only on your documented instructions; keep processing personnel bound by confidentiality; implement the technical and organisational measures described in the [security overview](/security); assist with data subject requests; and make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance.

### 4. Subprocessors

You authorise the subprocessors listed on the [subprocessors page](/legal/subprocessors). We give at least 30 days' notice of material additions, during which you may object on reasonable data-protection grounds; if we cannot resolve the objection you may terminate the affected service without penalty.

### 5. Breach notification

We notify you without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting your data, with the information available at the time and updates as the investigation proceeds.

### 6. International transfers

Where processing involves a transfer out of the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two) apply and are incorporated by reference, with the UK Addendum where relevant.

### 7. Deletion and return

On termination, we delete or return personal data within 90 days, except where retention is required by law. Backups age out within a further 30 days.

### 8. Audit

Once per twelve months, on reasonable notice, you may request our current security documentation and a written response to a security questionnaire. On-site audits are available on Enterprise.
