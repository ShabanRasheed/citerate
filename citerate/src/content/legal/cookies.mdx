### Cookies we set

| Name | Purpose | Type | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cr_session` | Keeps you signed in | Strictly necessary, httpOnly | 30 days |
| `cr_device` | Remembers a trusted device for 2FA | Strictly necessary, httpOnly | 90 days |
| `cr_prefs` | Remembers UI preferences (e.g. billing interval) | Functional | 1 year |
| `cf_clearance` | Cloudflare Turnstile / bot protection | Strictly necessary | Session |

### What we do not set

No advertising cookies, no cross-site trackers, no third-party pixels. Our analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) is cookieless and aggregates page views without identifying visitors — which is why this site shows a one-time notice rather than a consent wall.

### Controlling cookies

Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent signing in. Functional cookies can be cleared at any time from your browser without affecting the product.
