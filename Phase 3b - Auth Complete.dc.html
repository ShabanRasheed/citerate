Two ways in doubles the attack surface. With a password enabled, the account has two credentials and the weaker one sets the floor — which is why "disable magic links" sits next to the password field, and why I'd default new accounts to links only.
Domain recovery beats support tickets but assumes DNS access. For agency accounts where the client owns DNS, recovery should fall back to an admin-confirmed manual check — say the word and I'll design that state.
Passkeys would replace both tracks with something stronger and shorter (one tap, no shared secret, phishing-resistant). If you're open to it I'd design that instead of the password track for v2.
