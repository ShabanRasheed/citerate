Board 01 · session zero

"What are we actually measuring?"

2 people · 90 min · the only board that mattered
traffic is down but rankings are FLAT — why??
count brand mentions?
✕ measures noise. being talked about ≠ being linked
track rank in the AI answer?
✕ there is no "position 3" in a paragraph
count CITATIONS. did the engine hand the user OUR link?
binary. per run. countable. ✓✓
↳ so the unit is a RUN
1 query × 1 engine × 1 time = 1 run
→ NEVER report a % without the runs!!
ask 1 engine 5×, get 5 different answers. so ONE answer proves nothing.
if it's a proportion over trials → we can put a CONFIDENCE INTERVAL on it
that's the whole product. "is this real or is it noise" — nobody else answers that
DECIDED 👍
rate = cited runs / runs
WHAT SURVIVED

The run as the atomic unit, the run-count-with-every-rate rule, and the Wilson interval — all three are now enforced in scoring.ts and in the design system as invariants.

WHY IT MATTERS LATER

Every hard product decision downstream — the within-noise label, the two-scan fix verification, immutable reports — is a consequence of choosing a countable unit on this board.

Board 06 · proving a fix

"Did it work?" is harder than it looks

they ship a change. now what?
rescan immediately → green tick
✕ one scan can't beat the noise. we'd be lying with a checkmark.
let the user mark it "worked"
✕ then the proof is their word, and it's worthless to their boss
✓ wait 2 scans. compare the BANDS either side of the deploy date.
bands separate → VERIFIED. bands overlap → "no measurable change"
the wait is 3–11 days. TELL THEM when they hit "shipped" — don't let them discover it
"no measurable change" ≠ FAILED. design that state as carefully as the win.
↳ and freeze the before/after as a PDF. THAT is what an agency sells.
WHAT SURVIVED

The two-scan verification rule, the no measurable change state, up-front latency disclosure (US-25), and the fix_proof snapshot kind in the schema.

Board 07 · the agency problem

One person, fourteen clients, ninety minutes

she does the SAME task 14×. optimise for the switch.
client 1
client 2
client 3

client 14
✕ switcher in Settings — that's 14 round trips through a menu
✕ separate login per client — absurd
✕ charge per seat — punishes her for having staff. she'll share one login instead
✓ switcher lives in the TOPBAR. type-ahead. shows each score inline so she can triage BEFORE clicking
✓ switching keeps her on the same route — she's doing the same job 14×
✓ seats UNLIMITED on Agency. meter the WORKSPACES instead.

WHAT SURVIVED

Topbar switcher (US-32), the client role (US-33), unlimited seats with workspaces as the Agency meter, and white-label PDFs on Scale+ with one non-removable footer line.

Board 08 · gating napkin math

What do we hold back, and what would be cruel to?

free tier: how much do we give away?
GIVE (free)
the real rate ✓
the run counts ✓
the cause mix ✓
the EVIDENCE ✓
25 queries, 2 engines
HOLD BACK
competitor NAMES
the fix queue
history / trend
PDF reports
daily refresh
rule: never gate the EVIDENCE. only the NAMES and the REMEDY.
if they can't verify our numbers for free, why would they pay for more of them?
BLUR the locked bit — don't hide it. show their own data through frosted glass. that IS the upsell.
over the limit → DON'T STOP SCANNING. a hole in the series is unrecoverable. bill the overage, keep measuring.
↳ add-ons buy VOLUME only. never a feature. keeps the tier story honest.
WHAT SURVIVED

The whole gating philosophy in plans.ts: 25 free queries with real evidence, competitor names and fix queue from Growth, the blur-not-hide Locked component, overage-not-lockout metering, and volume-only add-ons.
